Your Privacy Matters

We believe in transparency. Please review our data handling practices below.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

analytics
Data We Collect (Analytics)

To improve Sapphire, we collect completely anonymous, aggregated usage data. This helps us understand which features are popular, identify bugs, and make the app better for everyone.

data_usage
What This Includes

  • check_circle Feature usage frequency (e.g., how often a widget is used)
  • check_circle App version and macOS version
  • check_circle Anonymous crash reports

block
Data We NEVER Collect

We are committed to your privacy. We DO NOT automatically collect, store, or transmit any personal or sensitive information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • cancel Your name, email, or other personal identifiers
  • cancel Screen contents or keyboard input
  • cancel Application data from other apps

cloud_off
Data Storage & Third Parties

Anonymous data is processed by Google for analytics. This data is always aggregated and cannot be used to identify you.

tune
Your Choice & Control

Sharing anonymous usage data with Google Analytics is optional and asked about during onboarding. You can change this anytime in the app under Settings > General.

language
Website & Payments

This website also uses Google Analytics to understand overall traffic in an aggregated form. Subscription payments and account data (email, license key, and billing information) are processed securely by Stripe and are never used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Questions about your privacy?

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, get in touch with us directly.
